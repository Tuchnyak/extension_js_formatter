# Privacy policy: Curly - JSON Formatter & Tree Viewer

Last updated: 2026-10-15

**Short version: the extension does not collect your data and does not send anything anywhere.**

## What the extension does with data

- It formats JSON entirely inside your browser. The JSON you open, paste or select is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the extension makes no network requests of its own.
- On web pages that are not JSON documents the extension does nothing and does not read the page.

## What is stored

- On your device only, in the browser's extension storage: your theme choice and the on/off state of automatic formatting.
- Briefly, in browser memory only: text you select and send to the viewer through the right-click menu. It is deleted as soon as the viewer tab reads it.
- Nothing you paste or view in the viewer is stored.

## What is not done

- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
- No accounts, cookies or identifiers.
- No sale, sharing or transfer of data to third parties; no third-party services or remote code.

## Permissions

The permissions and the reason for each are described in the store listing and in the project source code (the manifest and the `store/permissions-justification.md` file).
